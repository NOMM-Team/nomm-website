## Manual actions required

Most Switch games are currently supported by NOMM.

What NOMM supports are standard mods, i.e. mods that can be interpreted directly by Switch emulators without having to have a dedicated specialised mod manager.

These generally come in the form of `exefs` or `romfs` mods.

NOMM also supports 1-click download from GameBanana. For now the only supported game for this is Mario Kart 8 Deluxe, but we are expecting 1-click support to expand soon to other games.
