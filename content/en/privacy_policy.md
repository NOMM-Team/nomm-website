# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 25, 2026

At NOMM ("we," "our," or "us"), privacy is a core priority. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to your privacy and explains that we do not collect any personal data when you visit our website.

## 1. No Data Collection
**We do not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data.** 

When you use our website, we do not track or request:
* **Personal Identifiers:** Such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account registrations.
* **Usage Data:** Such as your IP address, browser type, device information, or access times.
* **Tracking & Analytics:** We do not use cookies, web beacons, tracking pixels, or telemetry to monitor your behavior.

## 2. Web Hosting & Infrastructure
Our website is hosted using **GitHub Pages**, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. 

When you visit any website hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may automatically collect server logs and basic network diagnostic data (such as your IP address and web browser user agent) to maintain the security, integrity, and performance of the hosting infrastructure. This data is handled directly by GitHub in accordance with the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement). We do not have access to these server logs, nor do we store or manage them.

## 3. No Advertising or Third-Party Tracking
Our website is completely ad-free. We do not integrate with:
* Third-party advertising networks.
* External analytics platforms (such as Google Analytics).
* Social media tracking widgets or plugins.
